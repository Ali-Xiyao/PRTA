> ablation and fusion comparator. Points show mean effects across the three
> fixed training seeds; bars show 95% percentile intervals over 10,000 paired
> patient resamples. Positive Macro-F1 values favor V2.

> Controlled PRIOR interventions reveal sensitivity to patient history. All
> conditions use identical patients, observations, targets, and frozen
> checkpoints. The interventions audit model behavior and do not establish
> clinical causality.
